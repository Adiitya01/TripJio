## 8. Booking, Acceptance and Cancellation

Booking: Load Owner submits a Request specifying pickup, drop, vehicle type, weight, and goods description.

Acceptance Window: Requests expire if not accepted by the Driver within 2 minutes of being sent.

Trip Formation: A Trip is formed when a Driver explicitly accepts a Request through the App.

Cancellation by Load Owner:

- Before Driver accepts: Free cancellation, no charge
- After Driver accepts but before Driver reaches pickup: A cancellation fee may apply (to be defined post-launch — currently no charge during Beta)
- After Driver reaches pickup: Trip Fare for the distance travelled is payable

Cancellation by Driver:

- Before reaching pickup: Permitted but may affect Driver's rating and standing on the Platform
- After reaching pickup or commencing Trip: Only permitted in exceptional circumstances (vehicle breakdown, accident, emergency); Driver must immediately notify the Load Owner through the App

Rate Limiting: To prevent abuse, Load Owners may not send more than 10 Requests per minute. Drivers may not toggle online/offline at unreasonable frequencies.

## 9. Trip Fare and Payment

The estimated Trip Fare is calculated based on:

- Base Fare (currently Rs. 50)
- Distance (Rs/km, varies by vehicle type — Mini Truck: Rs. 12, LCV: Rs. 16, HCV: Rs. 22, Container: Rs. 28)
- Weight Surcharge of Rs. 10 per tonne per km beyond the first 500 kg
- Any applicable taxes (GST), tolls, parking, or state-entry permits actually incurred

The fare shown in the App is an estimate. The final fare is the amount mutually agreed between Driver and Load Owner upon Trip completion.

Payment Mode: Initially, Trip Fare is to be paid directly in cash to the Driver at Trip completion. Trip-Jio may introduce in-app payment, wallet, or UPI in the future.

Platform Commission: Trip-Jio currently charges no commission during the Beta period. A commission structure will be introduced with 30 days prior notice to users.
