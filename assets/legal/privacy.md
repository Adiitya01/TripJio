# TripJio — Privacy Policy (Draft)

Effective Date: 13 August 2026
Last Updated: 13 August 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Trip-Jio Logistics (“Trip-Jio”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares your personal information when you use the Trip-Jio mobile application (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

- **Account information:** name, mobile phone number, role (Driver or Load Owner), city, and company name (Load Owners).
- **Driver verification information:** driver licence number, years of driving experience, vehicle number, and vehicle type.
- **Location information:** precise GPS location while the App is running, and at regular intervals while a trip is active, including when the App is running in the background. Background location is required to keep the Load Owner informed of the Driver’s progress.
- **Trip information:** pickup and drop locations, goods description, weight, trip status, timestamps, and the route travelled.
- **Device information:** device model, OS version, app version, and unique installation identifiers, used for analytics and crash reporting.
- **Communication metadata:** records of in-app calls or SMS triggered through the App (timestamps only — content is not recorded).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Operate the platform (match Load Owners with Drivers, dispatch trips, calculate fares).
- Provide real-time trip tracking and ETAs.
- Verify Driver identity and vehicle documents.
- Send transactional notifications (trip accepted, driver arrived, trip completed).
- Improve the App’s reliability via aggregated, de-identified analytics.
- Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We share information only as needed to operate the platform:

- **With the counterparty on a trip:** Driver name, vehicle number, phone, and live location are shared with the Load Owner for the duration of the trip. Load Owner name and phone are shared with the assigned Driver.
- **With service providers:** Google Maps and Firebase (Google LLC) for maps and notifications; Supabase (Supabase Inc.) for backend storage. These providers process data on our behalf.
- **With authorities:** when required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Trip-Jio or others.

We do **not** sell your personal information to advertisers.

## 5. Location Data and Background Access

The App requests background location permission to broadcast the Driver’s position during an active trip. Location is collected only while a trip is in `accepted` or `in_progress` state. When you go offline or no trip is active, background location collection stops.

You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. Doing so will prevent the App from offering or completing trips.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain trip records, including pickup/drop locations and route traces, for as long as your account exists, to support history and dispute resolution. If you delete your account, all personal information is deleted within 30 days, except records we are legally required to retain.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information (currently, by contacting support).
- Delete your account from inside the App (Profile → Delete Account). Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
- Withdraw consent for non-essential processing.

## 8. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard practices: encrypted connections (TLS), restricted database access (row-level security), and least-privilege backend roles. No system is perfectly secure; if you suspect a breach, contact us immediately at tripjio@gmail.com.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
