Delete Your TripJio Account

Complete instructions for permanently deleting your TripJio account and associated data.

TripJio (operated by APS Consulting Solutions) respects your right to delete your account and personal data at any time. You have two options:

Option 1 — Delete from within the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the TripJio app on your Android device
  2. Sign in with your registered phone number
  3. Tap the Profile icon (top-right corner of the home screen)
  4. Tap Account Settings
  5. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  6. Confirm your choice by tapping Yes, Delete in the confirmation dialog

Your account and all associated data will be deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app or your phone number, you can request account deletion by email:

tripjio.app@gmail.com

In your email, please include:

  • Subject: "TripJio Account Deletion Request"
  • The registered phone number associated with your account (including country code, e.g. +91XXXXXXXXXX)
  • Your full name as registered
  • Your role (Driver or Load Owner)
  • A brief statement confirming you wish to delete your account

We will verify your identity via an OTP sent to your registered number, then permanently delete your account within 7 business days of verification. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days:

  • Your profile information (name, phone number, city, company name)
  • Driver verification details (name, age, licence number, vehicle information)
  • All location history and GPS traces
  • All in-app messages and communication metadata
  • Firebase authentication account
  • Session and device information
  • Push notification tokens

What Data May Be Retained

Some data must be retained for legal, tax, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes:

  • Completed trip records (retained for up to 7 years for GST and tax compliance under Indian law). Personal identifiers are pseudonymised.
  • Financial/settlement records where applicable (retained as required by the Income Tax Act, 1961)
  • Support tickets and dispute records (retained for 1 year for audit and dispute resolution)
  • Anonymised analytics (retained indefinitely, contains no personal identifiers)
  • Records required by law enforcement, regulatory, or court order
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to all your trip history, ratings, and any pending trips. If you wish to use TripJio again, you will need to register from scratch.

Partial Data Deletion

If you would like to delete only specific categories of data (for example, only your location history) without deleting your entire account, please email tripjio.app@gmail.com specifying which data category you want removed. We will respond within 7 business days.
